How reach works

Reach

Reach counts countries where a video charted in the selected period. A video that charted in 10 countries has a reach of 10. Each country counts once, even if the video appeared on many days.

Weighted reach

Weighted reach gives more weight to higher chart positions. Each country contributes once, using the video’s best rank in the selected period: #1 adds 1 point, #50 adds 0.51, and #100 adds 0.01. Add those contributions for the total.

Per country: (101 − best rank) ÷ 100.

Both scores measure chart visibility, not audience size. Missing observations can reduce measured reach.

About the data

The data shown here comes from the publicly available YouTube Data API and is collected daily.

We include records from July 24, 2025 onward. YouTube changed how it populates its charts that month, making earlier records unsuitable for direct comparison.

This report uses Dec 1, 2025 – Dec 31, 2025. Its calendar window remains fixed; later source snapshots can revise metadata or observations. The data snapshot identifies the collection used for this report. Video histories cover the full available collection.

Only videos identified as Music (category 10) in the supplied metadata are included. Filtering does not renumber original chart positions. Publication dates describe the recorded release and do not determine which month a video appears in.

Excluded from the music ranking: 39,127 observed videos in other categories and 0 with unknown category or missing video metadata. Unknown channel origins remain eligible.

Reach ties use observed appearance days, then video ID. Weighted-reach ties use reach, observed appearance days, then video ID.

Every date in this month has at least one recorded chart; destination coverage can still be partial.

475 recorded charts contained fewer than 100 entries. Missing charts are not filled with repeated observations or zeros. Collection can skip unchanged charts, and chart absence does not establish an absence of viewers.

Titles and publication dates come from the latest supplied metadata. Channel-origin evidence and researched corrections remain available in each video's chart history.
